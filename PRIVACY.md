# LANPilot Audit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

LANPilot Audit is a local-first macOS network governance and reliability tool.
It is designed for authorized review of networks that the user owns or is
permitted to assess.

## Data Collection

LANPilot Audit does not collect personal data for the developer.

The app does not upload audit evidence, reports, logs, device inventory, network
observations, exports, or local files to a cloud service operated by the
developer.

## Local Data

Audit workspaces, generated reports, and exported ZIP files remain on the
user's Mac unless the user chooses to share them outside the app.

Local network observations may include information such as reachable addresses,
service exposure, gateway health, DNS behavior, and diagnostic timing. These
observations are used only to show results inside the app and to generate local
reports.

## Network Access

Network checks are user initiated. The app uses local network access to inspect
reachable devices, gateway, DNS, and connection health on networks the user owns
or is authorized to review.

The app does not test passwords, perform unauthorized login, exploit devices,
brute force services, or change network configuration.

## Accounts and Credentials

LANPilot Audit does not require an account. It does not request, store, or test
network, device, or service credentials.

## Third-Party Services

The App Store build does not upload audit data to third-party analytics or
tracking services.

Some diagnostics may send standard network requests selected by the user, such
as DNS, ICMP, TCP reachability, or NTP checks. These requests are part of the
diagnostic workflow and do not include audit reports or user files.
